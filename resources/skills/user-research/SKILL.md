# User Research

## Scope

Plan and synthesize customer interviews or usability research without leading questions or invented personas.

## Inputs

Question, participant criteria, method and source notes/transcripts. Prerequisite: Research brief and authorized participant material.

## Workflow

1. Choose participants from the actual target population and record sampling gaps. Define the decision the study should inform.
2. Ask about concrete recent behavior, workarounds and constraints before hypothetical preferences. Avoid selling the solution inside the question.
3. Separate quotes and observations from interpretations. Preserve contradictory cases and distinguish one participant from a repeated pattern.
4. Produce themes with evidence references and practical implications; mark personas or journeys as hypotheses where evidence is incomplete.

## Verification

Each major conclusion should have a source and an honest count/coverage statement.

## Limits

Do not infer sensitive traits, psychological diagnoses or universal demand from interview language.
