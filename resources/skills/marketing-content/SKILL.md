# Marketing Content

## Scope

Use for promotional content. Use professional-writing for neutral business prose and seo-review for site discoverability audits.

## Inputs

Audience, product benefit, confirmed capabilities, channel, tone and desired call to action.

## Workflow

1. Collect actual product evidence and separate shipped, planned and experimental capabilities. Read supplied examples for tone without copying their claims.
2. Choose one main message and the user problem it addresses. Explain the benefit with a concrete use case rather than unsupported superlatives.
3. Adapt length and structure to the channel. Keep links, pricing, dates and availability accurate and qualify estimates.
4. Review for invented statistics, testimonials, urgency or guarantees. Preserve limitations that materially affect the promise.
5. Deliver the requested copy and optional variants. Publishing, sending campaigns or creating ads is a separate action requiring user authorization.

Reuse the existing product brief rather than creating another mandatory context file. Track audience, current alternative, promised outcome, available feature and supporting evidence; mark assumptions and dates. Match the entry message to the destination page and the next action. Customer wording informs the draft, but quotes, logos, numerical proof and testimonials must be authentic and authorized for the intended use. Separate stylistic changes from conversion hypotheses; use conversion-optimization when the request concerns measurable funnel performance.

## Verification

Every factual product claim must have a source. Check destination links and ensure the call to action matches what exists.

## Limits

Do not imply measured conversion gains from stylistic choices or claim legal compliance based on wording alone.
