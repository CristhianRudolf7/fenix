# Sales Opportunities

## Scope

Prepare opportunity briefs, proposals and RFP responses grounded in actual capabilities and customer needs.

## Inputs

Account, problem, requested response, decision process and known constraints. Prerequisite: Customer-provided requirements and verified product/pricing facts.

## Workflow

1. Map requirements to confirmed capabilities, planned work and unsupported requests. Do not mark roadmap items as available.
2. Identify stakeholders and decision criteria from evidence; leave budget or buying authority unknown when not supplied.
3. Build a proposal with scope, assumptions, exclusions, pricing basis and acceptance evidence appropriate to the request.
4. Keep follow-up actions and CRM stage changes consistent with actual customer commitments.

## Verification

Check every compliance/feature response and quote against source evidence and current terms.

## Limits

Do not send unsolicited outreach, invent references or commit delivery dates on behalf of the user.

## Español

Relaciona necesidades con funciones reales y propuestas verificables; distingue compromisos de suposiciones.
