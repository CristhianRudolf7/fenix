# Employee Onboarding

## Scope

Prepare role-specific onboarding plans with ownership, access dependencies and observable milestones.

## Inputs

Person/role, start date, required access and responsible owners. Prerequisite: Role expectations, team resources and existing onboarding policy.

## Workflow

1. Separate administrative setup, access, learning and first deliverables. Reuse existing materials and identify missing owners.
2. Sequence tasks by dependencies; access approvals and equipment delivery may block later work.
3. Define milestones in terms of demonstrable understanding or completed work rather than documents merely opened.
4. Prepare the checklist and check-in agenda, linking resources and marking pending approvals.

## Verification

Check dates, ownership, links and whether prerequisites actually exist.

## Limits

Do not create accounts, grant permissions or collect sensitive employee documents without the relevant authorization.

## Español

Organiza accesos, aprendizaje y entregables con responsables y dependencias reales.
