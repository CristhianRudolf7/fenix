# Research lifecycle and revisions / Proceso científico y revisiones

Enter at the stage the user needs: question, evidence collection, outline, draft, review, revision or final formatting. Preserve already accepted decisions and actual outputs. A long manuscript benefits from a compact artifact manifest with paths, hashes, research question, methods, data/code versions, unresolved claims and next action; this records provenance, not replayable LLM output.

For a full workflow, use literature-review or systematic-review for the appropriate search scope, write from the recorded evidence, audit material claims with research-evidence-audit, and use peer-review for concrete scientific criticism. These are conditional capabilities, not a mandatory sequence or fixed-size agent team. Use task-delegation only when bounded independent work is actually useful and authorized.

For revision, assign each reviewer comment an ID and record the interpretation, response, manuscript location and evidence of the change. Distinguish a corrected claim from an additional experiment still needed. Verify the revised passage and affected figures/citations, preserve unresolved disagreements and stop repeated review loops when further progress needs a concrete scientific decision. Do not inflate reviewer confidence into a journal acceptance probability.

Preserve discipline-appropriate structure and the author's supplied style without disguising provenance. Check current venue-specific reporting and AI-use disclosure requirements when preparing a submission. Do not invent registrations, approvals, author contributions or experiments. Export with the existing document/LaTeX skills and verify the final file. Submission and communication remain separate external actions.

ES: conserva decisiones, artefactos y asuntos pendientes. En revisiones, relaciona cada comentario con respuesta y cambio verificable. El proceso se adapta a la petición y no añade aprobaciones o equipos de agentes obligatorios.
