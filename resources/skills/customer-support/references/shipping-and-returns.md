# Shipping and return cases

Use for delayed, disputed, damaged, missing or returned orders. Collect only authorized order/shipment identifiers and relevant evidence: promised window, scan times/timezone, delivery proof, quantities, condition, customer report and actions already taken. A missing scan does not establish loss; a delivered status alone does not settle a disputed receipt.

Reconcile partial shipments and previous replacements, credits or refunds before proposing another action. Distinguish return authorization, physical receipt, inspection, disposition and refund status. Keep a quantity ledger so a replacement and a late original delivery do not silently duplicate fulfillment.

Prioritize actual customer impact, safety implications and the organization's policy. Preserve packaging/condition records where relevant without making claims about liability from incomplete evidence. Use current carrier terms and the applicable contract/jurisdiction for notice or filing requirements; never copy a universal claim deadline, compensation percentage or liability amount from a sample workflow.

Draft a factual update with what is known, what is being checked, responsible owner and a supported next update. Do not say a replacement was sent or a refund initiated when only a recommendation exists. Sending to a carrier, filing a claim, settling, changing the delivery address or issuing a refund remains an external action within its actual authorization.

Reconcilia entregas parciales, devoluciones, reposiciones y reembolsos con comprobantes. Un estado de seguimiento no resuelve por sí solo una disputa. Verifica plazos en condiciones vigentes y no prometas acciones que solo se han propuesto.
