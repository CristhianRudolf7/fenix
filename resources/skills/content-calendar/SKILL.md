# Content Calendar

## Scope

Plan and prepare a coherent publishing calendar with real assets, owners and channel requirements.

## Inputs

Audience, period, cadence, topics, assets and approval/publishing workflow. Prerequisite: Editorial goals, existing content and channel constraints.

## Workflow

1. Inventory available and planned content; separate reusable source material from claims that need verification.
2. Assign each item a purpose, channel, date/timezone, owner and asset/dependency status. Avoid repeating the same message without a reason.
3. Adapt copy and format to each channel while preserving factual consistency and source links.
4. Deliver a calendar and ready drafts with blockers visible; schedule or publish only within explicit authorization.

## Verification

Check dates, links, asset readiness and whether the cadence is feasible.

## Limits

Do not create recurring posts, send messages or claim publication from a draft calendar.
