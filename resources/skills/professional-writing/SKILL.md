# Professional Writing

## Scope

Use for prose and business communication. Use technical-documentation for developer instructions and marketing-content for promotional copy.

## Inputs

Audience, purpose, supplied facts, desired length, language and template if any.

## Workflow

1. Extract the message the reader must understand and the action requested. Reuse known context; ask only when missing information would change the substance.
2. Create an outline suited to the deliverable, then draft in plain language. Keep facts, interpretations and proposed next steps distinguishable.
3. Use specific evidence in place of vague claims. Preserve names, figures, qualifications and uncertainty when shortening text.
4. Edit repetition and awkward rhythm without imposing a persona. Keep useful technical terms for expert readers and explain them for other audiences.
5. Compare the result to source material and provide the revised artifact. Sending or publishing is not implied by drafting.

Use scientific-writing when reporting methods/results, technical-documentation for developer usage, and contract-review for clause analysis. Preserve the requested output type rather than turning ordinary writing into a specialist workflow.

## Verification

Check factual fidelity, required sections, links, numeric consistency and whether the reader can act without hidden context.

## Limits

Do not invent testimonials, citations, achievements or guarantees. Do not promise that writing will evade AI detectors.

## Español

Redacta para un lector y propósito concretos. Mejora claridad sin alterar hechos, cifras ni incertidumbre; entregar un borrador no equivale a publicarlo.
