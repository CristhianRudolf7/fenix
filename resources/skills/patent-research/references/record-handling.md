# Patent record handling

Start from the official portal for the relevant jurisdiction and verify current access conditions. Useful entrypoints, checked on 2026-09-21:

- [USPTO patent search](https://www.uspto.gov/patents/search) for United States search options and links to official record systems.
- [WIPO PATENTSCOPE](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/patentscope) for its searchable patent-document collections; collection coverage must be checked for the actual query.
- [European Patent Register](https://www.epo.org/en/searching-for-patents/legal/register) for published procedural information within its stated scope.

Use discovery indexes to locate documents, then verify consequential assertions with official records. No single portal should be assumed to cover all countries, publication periods or unpublished applications. Authentication and bulk-download rules vary; discover their current documentation rather than assuming an API schema.

Maintain rows with source URL, searched-at time, exact query or identifier, filters, publication/application/grant ID, jurisdiction, kind code, priority/filing/publication dates, family grouping rule, passage/page and observed status date. Keep unknown fields empty and explained. Link alternate versions rather than silently replacing one document with another.

For technical comparison, list the requested feature, candidate document, supporting passage, what the passage actually discloses, and remaining ambiguity. Do not turn a partly matching feature table into a novelty or infringement opinion. Preserve original terminology alongside translated terms when translation could change meaning.

## Español

Elige fuentes oficiales por jurisdicción y registra consulta, fecha, identificadores y pasajes. Conserva versiones y miembros de familia distintos. Los portales y estados tienen cobertura limitada; una tabla técnica no es un dictamen jurídico.
