# Privacy And Compliance

## Scope

Map data practices and compliance evidence into concrete gaps without claiming certification.

## Inputs

Jurisdiction, data categories, actors, purposes, retention and review scope. Prerequisite: Actual processing inventory and current authoritative requirements.

## Workflow

1. Map data collection, use, sharing, storage and deletion against observed implementation. Distinguish a policy statement from a working control.
2. Identify the applicable obligations using current official sources; preserve jurisdiction and applicability assumptions.
3. Build an evidence register for access, retention, consent/other grounds, rights requests and vendor relationships as applicable.
4. Propose targeted fixes and document unresolved questions; draft notices that accurately describe actual practices.

## Verification

Link each claimed control to evidence and each gap to a requirement or stated policy.

## Limits

Do not declare GDPR/SOC/ISO compliance from a checklist or invent legal bases, approvals or certifications.

## Español

Relaciona prácticas reales con obligaciones vigentes y evidencia; una checklist no equivale a certificación.
