# Competitive Research

## Scope

Compare products and market alternatives with current evidence and explicit comparison criteria.

## Inputs

Target users, alternatives, geography, date and decision criteria. Prerequisite: Primary public sources or supplied market evidence.

## Workflow

1. Include direct competitors and practical substitutes. Define comparable plans/use cases before collecting prices or capabilities.
2. Use primary product/pricing documentation and record dates, currencies and plan restrictions. Distinguish marketing claims from demonstrated features.
3. For market sizing, state population, eligibility, spend and adoption assumptions separately; avoid treating total industry revenue as reachable demand.
4. Deliver a comparison and uncertainties tied to the user’s decision, including conditions that would change the recommendation.

## Verification

Check each material claim against its source and reconcile inconsistent plan definitions.

## Limits

Do not pose as a customer to obtain private information or invent market-share numbers.
