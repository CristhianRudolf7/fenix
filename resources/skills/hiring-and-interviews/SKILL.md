# Hiring And Interviews

## Scope

Prepare role criteria, structured interview guides and evidence-based candidate summaries.

## Inputs

Role, relevant competencies, interview stage and requested artifact. Prerequisite: Job requirements and user-supplied candidate evidence.

## Workflow

1. Derive criteria from actual job responsibilities and apply the same rubric consistently across candidates.
2. Prepare questions or work samples that reveal relevant capability, with anchored evaluation notes and reasonable accommodations.
3. Summarize candidate evidence without inventing achievements or inferring protected/sensitive characteristics.
4. For resume edits, preserve truthful experience and distinguish suggested wording from facts that need confirmation.

## Verification

Check every assessment against supplied evidence and clearly separate missing information from negative evidence.

## Limits

Do not autonomously reject/hire candidates or rank them using sensitive traits or unsupported personality inference.
