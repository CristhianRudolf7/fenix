# Grant Proposals

## Scope

Prepare funding proposals aligned with the actual call, evidence and a feasible budget.

## Inputs

Eligibility, aims, evidence, team, timeline, budget and required sections. Prerequisite: Current funding call and supplied institutional/project facts.

## Workflow

1. Read the current call and build a requirements/deadline checklist with timezone and submission constraints.
2. Connect each aim to a method, deliverable, risk and evaluation approach. Distinguish preliminary evidence from work proposed.
3. Reconcile staffing, equipment and indirect costs with permitted budget rules; label quotes and estimates by date.
4. Prepare the narrative and attachments without inventing commitments, partner letters or approvals.

## Verification

Check eligibility, page limits, arithmetic, consistency between aims and budget, and missing attachments.

## Limits

Submission and institutional signatures require the appropriate authorization; a polished proposal does not imply eligibility or funding.
